The 5-Step In-Depth Research Framework

Work in depth with topics we have already covered or topics you feel passionate about. Follow these five consecutive stages to prepare your thesis, research, and oral defense.

Step 01 · Topic & Prior Knowledge Activation

Individually or in pairs, choose a topic.

Review the work you have done connected to this topic to activate your background knowledge and skills. Choose something in the Health and Life Skills arena you are passionately curious about, or extend a concept explored during classroom lectures.

Teacher Recommendation: Selecting a topic where you have personal interest or strong curiosity ensures that deep reading and diverse source collection remains stimulating.
Step 02 · Formulation of Research Question

Choose Path A (Formulate Your Own Question) or Path B (Pick a Ready-Made Question)

Decide whether you want to formulate your own research question, or work with a ready-made research question from our catalog of 22 topics.
Important Principle: Your question should be answered by you at the start or at the end of the presentation. In effect, your whole presentation is actually an answer or an argument that supports this question you pose.

Time Constraint: Please remember that your presentation should not exceed 10 minutes. This time limit is designed to ensure you present clearly and concisely while allowing for audience engagement.
Step 03 · Format Selection & OneNote Documentation

Decide How You Want to Present Your Results

Select from our approved presentation formats (video, PowerPoint Presentation, PechaKucha , interview, demonstration, Ignite Talks , digital storytelling, debate, pitch, student-curated exhibition, lesson, TedTalk ).

Process Documentation in OneNote (Weekly): "In this assignment, I will be tracking each student's progress, which means you need to create a dedicated page in your OneNote to document your journey. This process is crucial because it allows me to see your development, understand your learning curve, and identify areas where you might need additional support. Document your work at least once a week."
Step 04 · Academic Integrity & Citing Orally

Master Citing Orally Throughout Your Delivery

Before going any further, read up on how to search for, evaluate, and cite your sources (see OneNote: "Citing orally" & "Giving presentations"/Citizens). This is fundamental to all research work.

• Create a full list of references and cite them orally throughout your presentation.
• Use our 8 attribution stems aloud: "According to...", "As stated by...", "In the words of..."
• Never hide your citations on the last slide alone.
Step 05 · Extensive Research with 5+ Credible Sources

Conduct Extensive Research & Build Your APA Reference List

When you have discussed your choices with your teacher, you must do extensive research using different written and/or oral sources (for example interviews or videos). You need to find at least five credible sources.

Citation Tool (BibGuru): "I suggest that you use BibGuru while creating your detailed list of references that are cited in your work in APA format."
Diverse Sources Mix: Incorporate scholarly journals alongside scientific podcasts, documentary clips, and expert interviews for varied evidentiary depth.

Interdisciplinary Mandate: Health and Life Skills Source: UDIR

The school's interdisciplinary topic health and life skills shall give you the competence which promotes sound physical and mental health, and which provides opportunities for making responsible life choices.

In the childhood and adolescent years, the development of a positive self-image and confident identity is particularly important. Relevant areas within this topic are physical and mental health, lifestyle habits, sexuality and gender, drug abuse, media use and consumption, and personal economy. Other issues include value choices, the importance of meaning in life, boundaries, and dealing with thoughts, feelings, and relationships.
